Observation Journal

1 In the 2/2 twill, does the diagonal line appear to advance or recede? What colour seems to sit on top?
2 Try a complementary pair (e.g. navy + orange). Describe what happens at the twill diagonal compared to plain weave.
3 Switch to M's and O's. Can you see the block structure clearly? What happens if you use very similar colours?
4 In the Rosepath structure, do the diamond figures advance or recede? Does value contrast or hue contrast have a stronger effect?
Suggested colour experiments:
What to observe:
  • Twill diagonal: With high value contrast the twill line appears to be a raised 3D rib
  • Block boundaries: In M's and O's the boundary between blocks is where simultaneous contrast is strongest — each block modifies the perceived colour of its neighbour
  • Figure/ground: In Rosepath, the diamond "figure" should advance with warm colours and recede with cool ones
  • Float length: Longer floats = larger colour areas = more true simultaneous contrast (less optical mixing)
Key insight: As you move from plain weave (1/1) to 3/1 twill to block weaves, the warp and weft colour areas grow larger. Larger areas → less optical mixing → more true simultaneous contrast. This is why 8-shaft block weaves produce the strongest colour contrast effects.
